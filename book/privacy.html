Calendar privacy

Booking information

This calendar is operated by Soham Bhattacharya. Guests do not need a Google account or Google authorization to book.

What the app uses

Your name, email address, meeting time, and optional notes are used to create a Google Calendar event and send its invitation, updates, cancellation notices, and optional reminder emails. Gmail sending permission is used only to send booking notifications, verification codes, and reminders from the host’s account; the app does not read the host’s inbox. Event details are stored in the host’s Google Calendar and shared with the host and invited attendees.

The app checks the host’s calendar to avoid conflicts. Visitors receive available time slots, not the host’s other event details.

Email verification and abuse prevention

Before a booking is created, we send a short-lived email verification code. We store hashed email identifiers, code hashes, and request counters to prevent abuse and enforce booking limits. Verification records are cleaned up after expiry. No calendar invitation is created until verification succeeds.

Booking records and private links

Cloudflare D1 stores booking identifiers, times, status, slot locks, private management tokens, and hashed reminder-recipient identifiers with delivery status. Keep your management link private: anyone with it can view, reschedule, or cancel that booking. Cancelled reservation records remain in the database to support consistent cancellation handling.

Administration uses a secure session cookie. Google authorization credentials stay on the server. The app does not use Google Calendar data for advertising or sell it.

Google access and requests

The host can revoke this app’s access in their Google Account’s third-party connections settings. To request deletion of a booking record or ask about this calendar, email sohamb117@gmail.com.
